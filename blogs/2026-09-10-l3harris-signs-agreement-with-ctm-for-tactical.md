L3Harris Technologies and Osrodek Badawczo – Rozwojowy Centrum Techniki Morskiej S.A. (CTM), a member of Polska Grupa Zbrojeniowa S.A. (PGZ), have signed a Memorandum of Understanding for the potential in-country production of a Polish WIDMO Combat Team Radio.
